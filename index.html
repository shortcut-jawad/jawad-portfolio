01 / Selected work at LAAM

Things I shipped that people now rely on.

LAAM is a fashion marketplace, and Octane is the platform that runs brand storefronts on top of it. My work sits across the whole stack: Nuxt apps, Python microservices, a Go cart service and the React Native app.

Flagship · 9 tickets A

Custom Size, end to end

Shoppers can now order a garment cut to their own measurements, and every one of those garments is traceable from checkout to the warehouse to the customer's door.

The problem

Every custom-cut unit shared the same SKU and barcode as every other unit of that product. Two garments cut for two different people looked identical at the warehouse. The measurements themselves were also being dropped on the way to the order.

What I built

  • Measurement entry on the product page, driven by each category's size-chart columns
  • Line-item attributes carried through the Go cart service and checkout onto the order
  • A database-enforced, unique SKU and barcode for every individual garment
  • Admin availability settings behind a Statsig gate, plus a seller "offer Custom Size" option
  • A seller measurements panel, a printable label, and audited corrections
  • A "Needs your attention" homepage row and a listing tag for sellers
  1. PDPmeasurements
  2. CartGo · sentinel
  3. Checkoutattributes
  4. OrderPostgres JSONB
  5. Garment IDSKU + barcode
  6. Sellerpanel · label

Shipped across 9 tickets and 3 services, and caught and fixed 8 silent defects on the way.

03 / How I work

Boring on purpose, careful by default.

01

Root cause first

I find out why something broke before I touch it. That habit is how the silent defects surfaced: a courier bill going to the wrong account, and SMS messages vanishing for local number formats.

02

Tests are the spec

Every pull request clears a 95%+ diff-coverage gate. I use Pytest, Vitest, Jest and Testcontainers, and mutation-test with Stryker.

03

Small, scoped PRs

One service per pull request, migrations that are safe to backfill, and rollouts behind feature flags.

04

PRD to production

I turn a product spec into tickets, map each one to the right repo and file, and then ship it all the way through.
